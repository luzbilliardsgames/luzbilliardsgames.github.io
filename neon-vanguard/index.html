Atravesse a cidade, derrote cada Overseer e roube suas armas, num jogo de ação clássico reconstruído com visual de console: cenários pintados, personagens 3D animados por captura de movimento e luzes dinâmicas.

The megacity has fallen to HELIX and its eight Overseers. You are KAI, a combat android with an arm cannon. Fight across the city, defeat every Overseer and take their weapons, in a classic action game rebuilt with console-quality visuals: painted worlds, motion-captured 3D characters and dynamic lighting.
