Privacy Policy — Rust Bucket Rally

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rust Bucket Rally (the "App"), a mobile game published by Luz Billiards Games ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information the App stores on your device

Your game progress (coins, records, upgrades, vehicles, start points, achievements, daily-reward streak) and settings (sound, music, language, graphics) are saved only on your device, in the App's private storage. We do not receive this information. It is deleted when you uninstall the App, and you can erase it at any time in Settings → Reset progress.

The App has no user accounts, no in-app purchases and no chat. We do not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK included in the App may collect and share with Google:

  • Device or other identifiers — the Android advertising ID (AD_ID) and similar identifiers;
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address;
  • App activity — interactions with ads (views, taps);
  • App information and performance — diagnostics and crash data of the ads SDK;
  • basic device information (model, operating system, language).

This data is sent encrypted (HTTPS). It is processed by Google under its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • AdMob data disclosure: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

When ads appear: never while you are driving. A banner may appear on the menu and garage screens, a full-screen ad may appear occasionally between runs, and the rewarded ad ("double your coins") is shown only when you tap it. Ad content is limited to a family-friendly maximum rating.

3. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and to fix problems, the App uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google LLC that processes data on our behalf. It collects:

  • App activity — game events such as starting and finishing a run (vehicle, start point, distance, coins, flips, duration, new record) and ad impressions;
  • Device or other identifiers — a random app-instance ID created by Firebase. The App does not let Firebase use the Android advertising ID or the Android ID;
  • Approximate location — country/region derived from your IP address (the IP address itself is not stored);
  • App information and performance — app version, sessions, first open, device model, operating system and language.

This data is sent encrypted (HTTPS), is not linked to your name or e-mail, is not used for ads personalization and reaches us only as statistics. It is processed under Google's policies: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Your choices and consent

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): before personalized ads are shown, the App displays Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ads (the button appears where consent applies).
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Deleting data: game data is removed by uninstalling the App or using Reset progress. Usage statistics are deleted automatically after up to 14 months; you can also ask us to delete them by e-mail. Data collected by Google for advertising is managed through your Google settings and the options above.

5. Children

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, is not part of the Google Play Families program, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App, contact us and we will help.

6. Security and retention

We do not operate servers of our own that store your data. Data on your device stays until you erase it or uninstall the App. Usage statistics are kept in Google Analytics for up to 14 months. Data collected by Google is retained under Google's policies.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App changes. The new version will be published at this same address with a new "Last updated" date before the change takes effect.

8. Contact

Luz Billiards Games — luzbilliardsgames@gmail.com

  • Localização aproximada — país/região derivados do endereço IP (o IP em si não é guardado);
  • Informações e desempenho do app — versão do app, sessões, primeira abertura, modelo do aparelho, sistema e idioma.

Esses dados são enviados criptografados (HTTPS), não são ligados ao seu nome ou e-mail, não são usados para personalizar anúncios e chegam até nós apenas como estatísticas. São tratados conforme as políticas do Google: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy e https://policies.google.com/privacy
